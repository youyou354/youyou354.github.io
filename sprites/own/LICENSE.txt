Everything in this folder is drawn for this site by tools/make_wildlife.py (no third-party art),
except that apple-tree.png / orange-tree.png start from the Bloomseed tree (see ../bloomseed/LICENSE.txt)
and add the fruit.
  deer, doe, rabbit, rabbit-white, rabbit-baby, fox - the wildlife
  barn - the red barn (doors open / shut)
  items - bag icons (carrot, wheat, apple, orange, milk, fish, heart)
  mushrooms - the forest's mushrooms
  birds-*, bees, windmill - tools/make_extras.py
